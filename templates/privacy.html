Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only what is needed to run the service:

  • Account details: your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data you enter: amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to create your account, let you sign in, and show you your own expenses, summaries and spending patterns. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database. Passwords are never stored in plain text. You can ask us to delete your account and expense data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may contact Google's servers when you visit the site. We do not share your account or expense data with any third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data removed, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
